As this World Cup trundles towards its inevitable denouement of an unholy desecration by Donald Trump. I have declared myself a football hipster and, as such, will only watch games broadcast on a smaller niche station in the UK, ITV4. As such, the World Cup will consist of only 5 games and run from tonight through Sunday. I am backing
- Bosnia-Herzegovina v Qatar - Bosnia to win.
- Paraguay v Australia - it is too late, I will be asleep, so no idea.
- Senegal v Iraq - close call, but I do think Senegal will do it.
- Cape Verde v Saudi Arabia - as it's late, I'll be asleep, so no idea. 
- Croatia v Ghana - very close to call. Ghana, because the American government has put them under special measures as a safety threat. 

I will likely see the winner of the FIFA World Cup in that 5-game spread, (well, three if you account for my sleep). I hope football is the winner.
